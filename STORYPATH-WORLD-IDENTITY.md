# Storypath World and Character Identity

> **Status:** Working canon during the Storypath rebuild  
> **Updated:** 6 October 2026

This document controls the current names and identity direction for Storypath while the experience and visual systems are rebuilt. It does not change the locked Standard 3 Mathematics curriculum architecture.

## Product

**Storypath** is a 2D Caribbean-inspired educational adventure where children learn curriculum concepts, experience why they matter, and use them to solve meaningful problems in an interactive living world.

Product principle: **Learn Along the Way.**

## First community

The working name of Storypath's first living community is **Paralin**.

Paralin is fictional. It should feel culturally authentic to Trinidad and Tobago and the wider Caribbean without reproducing a real town. Its environmental DNA may draw subtly from places meaningful to Storypath's founders, including **Arouca, Gasparillo, and Lopinot**.

The previous names **Kairana**, **Arouca Grove**, and the code spelling **Arouca Groove** are superseded as current world canon. They remain historical design references only.

The rebuild does not currently require a country layer above Paralin. Whether Storypath ultimately needs a country, multiple communities, islands, or another world-navigation hierarchy remains an open product-design decision.

## Core child characters

The two working protagonists are:

- **Aaliyah**
- **Theo**

Their initials quietly preserve the founder connection **A + T**. This is an internal origin detail, not required public-facing lore.

Aaliyah and Theo are new Storypath characters. They must not be treated as simple renames or reskins of Niko and Zuri.

### Preserved companion principle

The strongest rule from the previous companion system remains:

> Supporting characters may begin, notice, question, react, suggest, make mistakes, and model revision. The learner makes the consequential choice.

Aaliyah and Theo must both be capable, fallible, curious children. Neither is the designated smart one, reckless one, correct one, comic relief, teacher, or answer dispenser.

Their detailed personalities, families, interests, visual identities, wardrobes, voices, and biographies will be developed afresh during the character-design phase.

## Recurring community cast

Storypath retains the **functional recurring cast structure** developed for Standard 3 Mathematics because those relationships help the curriculum feel embedded in a living community.

Previous adult names and visual identities are reopened for redesign. Preserve roles and mathematical/story functions before assigning final names, biographies, or artwork.

Useful recurring functions include:

- teaching and guided learning;
- records, books, information, and classification;
- shop, market, exchange, money, and supplies;
- food, growing, recipes, quantities, mass, capacity, and time;
- making, fabric, patterns, symmetry, and design;
- building, shape, length, perimeter, and area;
- delivery, routes, grouping, rates, and logistics;
- sport, recreation, schedules, scores, and data;
- community inquiry, reporting, communication, and decision-making.

Characters should connect multiple places and stories rather than existing as one-off lesson NPCs.

## Founder Easter eggs

Paralin may contain subtle founder-inspired Easter eggs throughout its world-building.

Potential sources include meaningful people, places, memories, family connections, dates, foods, sayings, interests, and shared experiences. Known inspiration includes Arouca, Gasparillo, Lopinot, Angelique, Tymea, Aviya, and Azariah.

Rules:

1. **Story first; Easter egg second.**
2. Easter eggs should enrich the world without requiring explanation.
3. Do not distort a character, location, curriculum experience, or cultural detail merely to include a reference.
4. Most references should remain invisible to players who do not know their origin.
5. Track intentional references in an internal Easter Egg Ledger as the world develops.
6. Personal inspiration does not make a fictional character a direct representation of a real person unless explicitly decided later.

## Mathematics architecture

The existing **18-stop Standard 3 Mathematics architecture remains protected**.

Paralin is the world in which those mathematical needs are experienced. Renaming the community, characters, or child-facing destinations must not silently change curriculum objectives, dependencies, transfer requirements, evidence requirements, or progression.

The locations should feel like parts of a connected community rather than eighteen curriculum chapters placed on a map.

## Open design decisions

Still intentionally open:

- final Aaliyah and Theo character bibles;
- adult cast names and identities;
- family and relationship network across Paralin;
- final child-facing names for learning locations;
- exact geography and districts within Paralin;
- whether a larger country/world layer is needed;
- navigation and map structure;
- visual identity, art direction, camera, layout, and UI;
- which founder Easter eggs become canon.

These should be solved through the Storypath rebuild rather than inherited automatically from earlier Bridgepath visual concepts.
